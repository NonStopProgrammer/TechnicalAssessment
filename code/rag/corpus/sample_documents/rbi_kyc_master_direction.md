# RBI Master Direction — Know Your Customer (KYC)

## 3. Customer Due Diligence

### 3.1 Baseline Customer Due Diligence
Regulated entities must undertake Customer Due Diligence (CDD) prior to establishing an
account-based relationship or executing an occasional transaction above the prescribed
threshold, and must not establish a relationship or carry out a transaction with a
customer whose identity has not been verified.

### 3.2 Enhanced Due Diligence Triggers
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is mandatory for customers and transactions assessed as high
risk, including but not limited to: cross-border wire transfers, transactions involving
jurisdictions identified by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) as high-risk, and
politically exposed persons (PEPs).

### 3.3 Cross-Border Transfers to Non-KYC-Verified Entities
A cross-border wire transfer to a beneficiary or counterparty whose KYC has not been
verified must not be processed; the transaction must be held pending verification. Any
such transaction above USD 1,000,000 (or equivalent) to a non-KYC-verified entity in a
jurisdiction rated high-risk by FATF must be escalated to the Money Laundering Reporting
Officer (MLRO) for a mandatory enhanced due diligence review before any funds are
released.

### 3.4 Unknown KYC Status
Where a customer's KYC status cannot be determined at the time of screening, the
transaction must be treated as unverified and rated no lower than MEDIUM risk pending
confirmation. An unknown KYC status must never be treated as compliant by default.
