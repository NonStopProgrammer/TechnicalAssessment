# RBI Master Direction — Priority Sector Lending (NBFCs)

## 5. Priority Sector Lending Targets and Reporting for NBFCs

### 5.1 Applicable Target
Non-Banking Financial Companies — Investment and Credit Companies (NBFC-ICCs) with an
asset size of ₹500 crore and above must ensure that Priority Sector Lending (PSL)
constitutes at least 40% of their Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC), computed as of the
corresponding date of the preceding financial year.

### 5.2 Qualifying Categories
Any lending transaction classified under agriculture, micro or small enterprises, export
credit, affordable housing, or another category specified in Annex I qualifies as Priority
Sector Lending and must be tagged as such at origination.

### 5.3 Reporting Obligation
NBFCs must submit a quarterly Priority Sector Lending return to the Reserve Bank in the
prescribed format. A lending transaction that qualifies as priority sector but is not
tagged and reported constitutes a reporting compliance gap and must be flagged for
remediation.

### 5.4 Shortfall Deposits
Any shortfall in achieving the applicable Priority Sector Lending target must be deposited
in the Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) or another fund as specified by the
Reserve Bank from time to time.
