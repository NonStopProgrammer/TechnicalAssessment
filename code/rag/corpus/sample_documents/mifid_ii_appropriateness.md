# MiFID II — Investor Protection and Appropriateness Assessment

## 25. Assessment of Suitability and Appropriateness

### 25.1 Appropriateness Assessment Requirement
When providing investment services other than investment advice or portfolio management,
a firm must ask a retail or professional client to provide information regarding their
knowledge and experience in the investment field relevant to the specific type of product
or service offered, so as to assess whether the product or service is appropriate for that
client (the "appropriateness assessment").

### 25.2 Definition of a Complex Product
A complex product is any financial instrument that does not meet the criteria for a
non-complex instrument, including but not limited to: derivatives, structured products,
and instruments embedding a derivative.

### 25.3 Insufficient Information
Where a firm considers, on the basis of the information received, that the product is not
appropriate for the client, the firm must warn the client. Where the client does not
provide the requested information, or provides insufficient information, the firm must
warn the client that it is not in a position to determine whether the product is
appropriate.

### 25.4 Prohibition Without Documented Assessment
A firm must not execute a transaction in a complex product for a retail client without
having first performed and documented the appropriateness assessment required under
clause 25.1, except where the transaction is executed solely at the initiative of the
client under the execution-only exemption, which itself requires specific conditions to be
met and separately documented.
