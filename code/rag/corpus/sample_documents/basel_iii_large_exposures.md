# Basel III — Large Exposures Framework

## 9. Large Exposures Framework

### 9.1 General Large Exposure Limit
A bank's exposure to a single counterparty, or a group of connected counterparties, must
not exceed 25% of the bank's Tier 1 capital (the "large exposure limit").

### 9.2 G-SIB Large Exposure Limit
For exposures between two global systemically important banks (G-SIBs), the large
exposure limit is reduced to 15% of Tier 1 capital.

### 9.3 Intra-Group Exposures
Intra-group exposures are subject to the same large exposure limit as third-party
exposures set out in clause 9.1, unless the group has been granted a specific waiver by
its home supervisor. Any such waiver must be documented and reviewed annually; in the
absence of a documented, current waiver, intra-group exposures must be measured and
reported against the clause 9.1 limit like any other exposure.

### 9.4 Breach Reporting and Escalation
A breach of the large exposure limit must be reported to the supervisor within 5 business
days of discovery and remediated within an agreed timeline. A wilful or repeated breach
must be escalated internally as a HIGH risk item pending regulatory review, regardless of
the size of the breach.
