# Basel III — Minimum Capital Requirements (2023 Amendment)

This amendment restates the 2019 minimum capital requirements (clauses 6.1-6.3, unchanged)
and introduces a Countercyclical Capital Buffer (new clause 6.5).

## 6. Minimum Capital Requirements

### 6.1 Common Equity Tier 1 Ratio
Banks must maintain a Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio of at least 4.5% of
risk-weighted assets (RWA) at all times. Unchanged from the 2019 requirement.

### 6.2 Tier 1 Capital Ratio
Tier 1 capital, comprised of Common Equity Tier 1 and Additional Tier 1 capital, must be
at least 6.0% of risk-weighted assets (RWA) at all times. Unchanged from the 2019
requirement.

### 6.3 Total Capital Ratio
Total capital, comprised of Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital, must be at least 8.0% of
risk-weighted assets (RWA) at all times. Unchanged from the 2019 requirement.

### 6.4 Capital Conservation Buffer
In addition to the minimum requirement in clause 6.1, banks must maintain a Capital
Conservation Buffer of 2.5% of risk-weighted assets, comprised of Common Equity Tier 1
capital. A bank whose capital falls within the buffer range is subject to constraints on
capital distributions (dividends, share buybacks, discretionary bonus payments). Unchanged
from the 2019 requirement.

### 6.5 Countercyclical Capital Buffer (New)
Effective January 2023, banks must additionally maintain a Countercyclical Capital Buffer
of between 0% and 2.5% of risk-weighted assets, as determined by the relevant national
regulator based on prevailing credit-growth conditions. Institutions must reassess their
buffer requirement whenever a national regulator publishes an updated buffer rate for a
jurisdiction in which they have credit exposures.
